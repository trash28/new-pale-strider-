# Music Asset Manifest — Pale Strider

All background music tracks in **Pale Strider** are genuine dark ambient, industrial drone, and atmospheric electronic recordings sourced from the **Internet Archive**, dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)**.

Every track has been downloaded, trimmed into a seamless loopable segment, high-pass filtered at 48 Hz (controlling excessive sub-bass), loudness-normalized to `-20 LUFS` (`TP=-3 dB`) with smooth fade-ins/fade-outs, and stored locally under `/public/assets/audio/music/` for 100% offline Web Audio API playback with smooth crossfade transitions.
