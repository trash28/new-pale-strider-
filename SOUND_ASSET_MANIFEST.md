# Sound Asset Manifest — Pale Strider

All external sound effects and environmental beds used in **Pale Strider** are genuine real-world recordings sourced from the **USC Cinema Sound Archive / Sunset Editorial "Red Library"** on the Internet Archive, dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)**.

Every clip has been trimmed, stripped of silence/album art metadata, peak-normalized (`loudnorm`), and stored locally under `/public/assets/audio/` for offline Web Audio API playback with multi-layer DSP spatialization, distance occlusion, and speed-of-sound delay.
